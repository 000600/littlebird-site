    Method

    How to set a Christmas gift budget

    Five steps, in order. The calculator above does the arithmetic for steps three and four.

    1. Start from what you can pay in cash

      Pick the total you can cover by the end of December without a balance hanging around into spring. Then fit the list to the number.

    2. List everyone, including the easy-to-forget

      Teachers, the host of the party you’re going to, the office gift swap, the neighbor who takes in your packages.

    3. Give each group one amount

      Deciding per group is quicker than per person, and it keeps things even between siblings and cousins.

    4. Leave room for the extras

      Wrapping paper, tape, cards, postage and stocking stuffers add up. We suggest a 10% cushion.

    5. Write prices down as you buy

      Spent and left only stay honest if each purchase is recorded the same day.

    Questions

    How much to spend on Christmas gifts

    How much should I spend on Christmas gifts?

    Spend what you can pay off by January without stress. For a reference point, the National Retail Federation’s 2025 holiday survey found shoppers planned to spend $627.93 on gifts on average, out of $890.49 for the whole season. A smaller budget spent on things people actually asked for goes a long way.

    How much should I spend per person?

    There’s no standard amount. A common approach is tiers: the most for a partner and children, a middle amount for parents and siblings, and one small, fixed amount for everyone else. Agreeing on a limit with siblings or a gift swap keeps it fair.

    What should a Christmas budget include besides gifts?

    Wrapping paper, tape, cards, postage, stocking stuffers, and the host gift you remember on the drive over. The calculator’s 10% cushion is there for these.

    How do I stick to my Christmas gift budget?

    Decide the amount for each person before you shop, buy from ideas you already have instead of browsing, and record each price as you buy. Ordering early also saves you from paying for rush shipping.

    Does this calculator save my numbers?

    Only in your own browser, so they’re still here when you come back. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there’s nothing to sign up for.
