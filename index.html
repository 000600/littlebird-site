Questions

Good to know

Is Little Bird free?

Yes. It’s free for up to four people and one gifting season, with unlimited hints and reminders. Plus unlocks everyone, every season, Idea Spark and export for $14.99 a year after a 14-day free trial, or $29.99 once.

Do the people I add know?

No. Nothing is shared or sent. It’s your private notebook, on your iPhone.

Can I import birthdays?

Yes, from Contacts. Only the people you choose are saved.

Does it work with Amazon, Etsy and Instagram?

Share any product page to Little Bird from the Share Sheet. It fills in the name, picture and price.

Why not just use the Notes app?

Notes doesn’t know anyone’s birthday, what you already bought, what you gave last year, or how much you’ve spent. Little Bird does — and reminds you two weeks before.

Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026. Little Bird collects nothing. There is no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising and no server that receives your data.

Where your data lives. People, hints, dates, budgets and gift history are stored on your iPhone, shared only with Little Bird’s own widget and Share extension. Your data is included in your device backups like any other app.

Contacts. If you import birthdays, Little Bird reads names and birthdays on-device and saves only the people you select.

Links. When you paste or share a link, your iPhone fetches that public page directly to read its title, image and price.

Idea Spark. Suggestions are generated by Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. Your notes never leave your iPhone.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see payment details. Delete a person or hint in the app, or delete the app, to remove everything.
