Privacy policy

Little Bird is built so the people you love are never a dataset.

What we collect

Nothing. Little Bird has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no server that receives your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — people, hints, dates, budgets and gift history — is stored on your iPhone, shared only with Little Bird’s own Home Screen widget and Share extension. Your data is included in your device backups like any other app.

Contacts

If you choose to import birthdays, Little Bird reads names and birthdays from your contacts on your iPhone and saves only the people you select. Nothing is uploaded.

Links

When you paste or share a link, your iPhone fetches that public page directly to read its title, picture and price. No one else sees that request.

Idea Spark

Suggestions are generated by Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. Your notes never leave your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. Little Bird never sees your payment details.

Children

Little Bird is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a person or a hint in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Questions

Reach us through the support page.
