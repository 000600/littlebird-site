Support

How can we help?

Little Bird keeps everything on your iPhone, so there’s no account to recover and nothing to log into.

Contact us

Open a request at github.com/000600/littlebird-site/issues. Please don’t include personal details about the people in your list.

Restore a purchase

Open Little Bird → Settings → Little Bird Plus, or the paywall, and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Little Bird Plus. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Reminders don’t arrive

In Little Bird, open Settings and turn on “Nudge me before each day.” If iOS asked earlier and you declined, allow notifications in the iPhone Settings app under Little Bird. Settings in the app shows your next scheduled nudge.

Save hints from Safari or shopping apps

Tap Share on any product page and choose Little Bird. If it isn’t in the row, scroll to the end and tap More to add it.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your hints come with your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). There is no separate account.
